Specialist welding recruitment for manufacturers: MIG, TIG, coded, pipe and structural welders, plus welding inspectors.

Candidate Requirements

  • Process-specific welding experience (MIG / TIG / Stick)
  • Positional welding ability where required
  • Blueprint and WPS reading
  • Shop-floor safety compliance

Certification Knowledge

Forge search consultants understand common welding qualification frameworks (e.g. AWS-style codes, ASME Section IX, API 1104 for pipeline work) and screen for relevant, current certification before presenting a candidate.

Employer Challenges We Solve For

  • Shortage of coded welders in the local labour market
  • Shift coverage gaps on multi-shift production lines
  • Certification lapses going unnoticed until audit
  • Demand spikes tied to project or seasonal work
